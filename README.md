# SQL-Bike-Sales-Customer-Insights
SQL-based customer and product sales analytics project using SQL Server. Analyzed customer purchasing behavior, sales performance, product performance, customer demographics, and customer segmentation to generate actionable business insights.

The project transforms transactional sales data into customer- and product-level insights using SQL, including aggregations, joins, CTEs, window functions, date calculations, and CASE-based segmentation.

### Customer Analysis

* Calculated total sales, orders, quantities, and distinct products purchased by each customer.
* Calculated customer age and grouped customers by age.
* Identified each customer's most recent order date.
* Segmented customers based on their spending behavior into New, Regular, and VIP customers.

### Product Analysis

* Analyzed product sales and performance.
* Grouped products by category and subcategory.
* Classified products based on their sales performance.
* Calculated sales-related metrics to identify high- and low-performing products.
